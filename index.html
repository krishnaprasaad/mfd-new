1. About Us

The Live Investments platform is accessible via the website www.liveinvestments.club and the Live Investments mobile application (collectively referred to as "the Platform"). The Platform is designed to facilitate investor access to mutual fund research recommendations, scheme information, and distribution services issued under AMFI Registered MFD ARN-300748 and APMI Registered PMS Distributor APRN 09608 — KRISHNA PRASAAD B.

KRISHNA PRASAAD B is the sole AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India) registered Mutual Fund Distributor holding ARN-300748 and APMI (Association of Portfolio Managers in India) registered PMS Distributor holding APRN 09608. The AMFI and APMI registrations are held exclusively by the Principal Officer and are used to operate the Live Investments platform.

We are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all information shared by our clients through the Platform, in full compliance with applicable SEBI/AMFI regulations and data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our wealth management services, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity Information: Full name, date of birth, PAN card number, Aadhaar number (where applicable)
  • Contact Information: Email address, mobile number, correspondence address
  • Financial Information: Bank account details, investment details, portfolio holdings, transaction history
  • KYC Documents: Identity proof, address proof, passport-size photographs as required by SEBI/AMFI regulations
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage data for security and analytics purposes

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:

  • Processing your investment transactions and orders
  • Completing mandatory KYC (Know Your Customer) verification as required by SEBI/AMFI
  • Maintaining your account and providing portfolio-related services
  • Sending essential communications such as transaction confirmations, account statements, and regulatory notices
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations under SEBI, AMFI, Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), and Income Tax laws
  • Responding to queries, grievances, or complaints raised by you
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring the security of your account

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Asset Management Companies (AMCs): To facilitate your investment transactions
  • Registrar & Transfer Agents (RTAs): Such as CAMS or KFintech, for transaction processing and account servicing
  • Regulatory Authorities: SEBI, AMFI, Income Tax Department, or other government authorities when required by law
  • KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs): For KYC verification and compliance
  • Technology Partners: Service providers who assist in operating our platform, under strict confidentiality agreements

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal and financial information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted through our website
  • Secure, encrypted storage of sensitive account credentials
  • HTTP-only session cookies to prevent unauthorized access
  • Regular security reviews and access controls
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your account credentials and report any suspicious activity immediately.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and for a period of 8 years thereafter, as required under PMLA and SEBI regulations, or as otherwise required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your account data subject to any mandatory regulatory retention requirements.

7. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses essential cookies strictly necessary for authentication and security purposes. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. By using our website, you consent to the use of these essential cookies. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, however this may affect certain functionalities of our platform.

8. Your Rights

As our client, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to regulatory retention requirements
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data for non-essential purposes

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@liveinvestments.in.

9. AMFI & SEBI Compliance

Live Investments operates in full compliance with SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, AMFI Code of Conduct, Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), and all applicable RBI and IRDAI guidelines. Our ARN registration (ARN-300748) and APMI registration (APRN 09608) are maintained current with AMFI and APMI respectively, and our Principal Officer KRISHNA PRASAAD B holds all necessary certifications as required by AMFI/APMI/NISM.

Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks. Please read all scheme related documents carefully before investing.
